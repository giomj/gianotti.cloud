Path to Rack Power · MG Program Roadmap

There is no upgrade from the MG-C1 hand-crank charger to a server rack — but there is a sequenced program path. It runs through MG-2's Pseudo Direct Drive, a real wind or water resource, and the program's own validation gates. Every step below respects the doctrine: the gear is a transmission; Pout ≤ Pin always.

MG-2 — Pseudo Direct Drive: merge gear and generator Next machine

Bond the low-speed magnet array into a stator bore and wind three phases around it: the gear's high-speed rotor becomes the generator's rotor. One housing, no mechanical teeth, and the pull-out slip becomes a built-in torque fuse. Torque density jumps from MG-1's 12–20 N·m/L to a validated 60–90 N·m/L (Magnomatics-class hardware: 500 kW wind PDD demonstrated at ORE Catapult; 4,000 N·m bus wheel motor).

MG-4 — demonstrator on a real resource Buildable class

Replace the human arm. The applications study rates micro-hydro as the best first target (very low RPM, high torque, wet environment where a sealed magnetic air gap shines) and small/distributed wind (1–20 kW) as buildable. An uncontrolled natural input is also the honest test of the torque fuse. Human cranking tops out near 54–68 W sustained regardless of gear size — no rack path exists through a crank.
